# TempleGap
Temple Gap Finder is a web app that helps Temple University students make the most of the free time between classes. You enter your weekly schedule (classes, events, meetings), and the app automatically finds the gaps in your day and suggests what to do with them.
Click any gap on your timeline and you get suggestions matched to how much time you have: a quick lunch spot if it’s around midday, a study spot like Charles Library or SERC for longer breaks, coffee for a medium one, or just a heads-up to get to your next class early if the gap is short. Each day shows how many classes and gaps you have, plus total free time.
Features
Weekly schedule builder for classes, events, and meetings, with copy-friendly day views
Automatic gap detection (15+ minutes) with a visual timeline
Context-aware suggestions based on gap length and time of day
Campus food finder with filters for price, meal plan acceptance, and dietary needs (halal, kosher, vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free), plus order-ahead links
Quick access to campus services (Career Center, Counseling, Writing Center), group fitness classes, and live events from Temple NOW
Campus map tab with Google Maps directions to major buildings, with optional location sharing
Schedule export/import as JSON, with everything saved locally in the browser
